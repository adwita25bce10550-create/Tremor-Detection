# Tremor-Detection
**what is its use**
This project of mine will help people suffering from tremors to detect tremors and people near to keep a track of tremor so that a proper action can be taken.

This project has taken a real life situation of Tremor.
**what is a tremor**
Tremors are involuntary, rhythmic muscle contractions causing shaking or trembling in parts of the body, most commonly the hands, but also the head, voice, or legs. They are symptoms rather than a disease, often caused by neurological disorders.
